# World Monitor: macro, finance, energy, trade, sanctions, markets, China, tech, resilience (slice B)

**Summary.** Covers all 202 manifest providers in `wm-B-macro.md` (179) and `wm-X-unclassified.md` (23). Every host is checked to appear below. After grouping hosts by provider, the counts are:
- **Keyless: 164.** That is 106 structured providers (Tables 1–10, about 14 of them scrape-only or blocked) plus 58 RSS/news publishers (Table 11).
- **Free key: 14.**
- **Paid: 3.**
- **Reference-only: 10** hosts that are not fetched (Table 12).

Keyed and paid providers are in Table 13. UN Comtrade and FRED each have both a keyless row and a keyed row.

There were **68 sequential polite probes**:
- 62 returned 2xx/3xx.
- 3 returned 404: Treasury FiscalData on WM's path, the FAO price-index CSV, and my WITS query.
- 1 returned 401: OpenSanctions API.
- 2 timed out: home.treasury.gov, and MINETUR mid-body on a 12 MB response.
Most rows are country-level series. The **Feeds feature** column says what each row can attach to in OSIRIS (country, place, company/CIK, vessel/IMO, port or commodity).

**Top 10 picks for OSIRIS** (keyless, licence usable or close, attachable to an entity or a place):
1. **OFAC Sanctions List Service** (SDN + Consolidated advanced XML): US public domain, keyless. It includes entities, vessels (IMO/MMSI), aircraft and addresses. It would replace the non-commercial OpenSanctions mirror OSIRIS uses now (DATA-SOURCES.md "Add these first" #3).
2. **SEC EDGAR** (live 8-K Atom stream, `company_tickers.json`, per-CIK submissions with HQ address, full-text search): public domain, needs a declared User-Agent. Gives a company backbone plus live corporate events.
3. **Spain fuel-station prices (MINETUR Geoportal)**: about 12k stations, each with a price and coordinates, refreshed often. A ready **point+ts** map layer and an energy-price signal. Mexico CRE (station XML) is a sibling source.
4. **INFORM Risk API (EC JRC)**: keyless JSON of country risk scores. It could replace the *editorial* baseline in `/api/country-risk`. Pairs with UNDP HDI and the FATF lists.
5. **World Bank WDI API**: CC BY 4.0 country indicators (reserves in months, electricity access, LPI, WGI governance, military spending) for `/api/region-dossier`.
6. **Public procurement notices**: TED v3 (EU), UK Contracts Finder (OCDS), World Bank procnotices, CanadaBuys CSV. All keyless. Buyer entity + country (+ NUTS/postcode), refreshed hourly. A new "events" family.
7. **US DoD MIRTA installations** (ArcGIS FeatureServer): public-domain base points and boundaries, already wanted in DATA-SOURCES.md.
8. **UNHCR ODP situations API**: daily arrival timeseries and per-host-country sublocation counts (CC BY 4.0 per WM). A humanitarian/displacement layer.
9. **Ember monthly electricity** (CC BY 4.0) **+ JODI oil/gas**: country-month energy generation, demand and stocks for energy-dependency context.
10. **USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries (+ BGS fill)**: public domain mine and refinery shares per country and commodity. Feeds supplier-concentration (HHI) risk in `/api/scm-suppliers`.

Honourable mentions: **Kalshi** public API (keyless; would slot into `src/lib/oi/markets.ts` next to Polymarket and Manifold; terms-review). **BIS / ECB / IMF SDMX** (keyless macro for the dossier). **CFTC COT** (public domain, weekly). **US Treasury FiscalData** (customs-revenue proxy for tariff impact).

**Licence risk of keyless market-quote fallbacks** (details in the notes after Table 13): WM relies on **Yahoo Finance** (v8 chart is keyless; v7/v10 need a cookie from `fc.yahoo.com` plus a crumb). Yahoo's ToS bars redistribution. WM itself is retiring Yahoo (#3731) and keeps only an owner-approved exception. OSIRIS's `/api/markets` and `oi` use the same endpoint, so this is a live risk for OSIRIS too. Other keyless quote paths in this slice: the **TradingView** undocumented scanner POST, scraped **Barchart** pages, the unofficial **CNN** Fear & Greed JSON, and the **SPDR GLD** XLSX (needs Origin/Referer). All are high ToS risk. WM does **not** use stooq. CoinGecko and CoinPaprika (crypto fallbacks) are in the A-geo chunk.

Probe legend: `206 … (ranged)` means a 4 KiB ranged GET of a bulk file, so the size is not the file size. Probes used UA `osiris-source-survey/0.1 (hello@takip.dev)`, one GET per provider, sequential.

**Non-UI feature ideas from this slice** (how WM does it, in prose)
- **Macro radar verdict.** Seven binary signals. JPY 30-day rate of change (liquidity). BTC vs QQQ 5-day return gap (flow alignment). QQQ vs XLP 20-day rate of change (risk-on regime). BTC vs SMA50 and 30-day VWAP (trend). Hashrate 30-day change (miners). BTC vs a fixed mining-cost threshold. Crypto Fear & Greed above 50. The verdict is BUY when at least 57% of the *known* signals are bullish; unknown signals leave the denominator. VWAP pairs price and volume only where both exist. Mayer multiple = price / SMA200.
- **BTC ETF flow estimate** without paid data: direction from the close-to-close change, conviction from volume relative to its trailing average, magnitude ≈ volume × price × direction × 0.1. WM labels it an approximation.
- **Stablecoin peg health:** within 0.5% = on peg, 0.5–1% = slight depeg, above 1% = depegged. The aggregate status is computed only over the coins returned, and coverage status is reported separately from peg health.
- **Prediction markets as early warning.** Flag a market whose probability moves before matching news volume arrives. Map countries to tag/name variants. Exclude sports/entertainment with keyword lists. Keep markets far enough from 50% or above $50k volume.
- **Sanctions pressure per country:** count OFAC entities by address country (ISO2) and feed the count into country risk.
- **FATF listing** and **BIS cross-border claims** are used as financial-exposure indicators. The **customs-revenue series** (Treasury MTS) is a near-real-time tariff-impact proxy because WTO tariff data lags about a year.
- **Supply vulnerability** per country × commodity, scored 0–100. C is source concentration (import-partner HHI plus mine/refinery HHI from USGS/BGS). T is chokepoint transit share; an active disruption multiplies it by 1.35. The base is 0.45C + 0.25T + 0.20(C×T), plus 0.10 × buffer vulnerability when stock data exists.
- **Five-factor scorecard:** absolute 1–5 scores (with a 0–100 sub-score) for food, energy, demographics, technology and defence self-sufficiency, built from annual structural data. The question it answers: "if cut off, what breaks first?".
- **Country resilience index:** 72 indicators in 21 dimensions and 6 domains. The indicator manifest (`docs/methodology/indicator-sources.yaml`) records each indicator's source, series id, licence and a "mechanism test", plus a constructStatus (observed / wealth-proxy / imputed / dead-signal). It is a useful checklist of free country indicators (WB WDI, WGI, LPI, WHO GHO, IPC, UNHCR, GPI, RSF, BIS, IMF, Comtrade HHI).
- **Physical precious-metals premium:** SGE PM print (CNY/g) × CNY/USD × 31.1034768 vs COMEX futures, keeping each leg's own observation time.
- **Procurement "automationFit":** a transparent keyword score with evidence, for tech-relevant tenders. Records without a verifiable closing date are never published.
- **Explicit missingness everywhere.** Each source has its own transport freshness and content freshness. States are available / partial / stale / unavailable / blocked. A missing value is never shown as zero. Last-good data keeps its original timestamp. Revisions are kept as vintages instead of overwriting.
- **China logistics corridors:** four reviewed polygons (YRD, GBA, Bohai, Western Land-Sea). Each is built from six evidence families (PortWatch ports, airports, hazards, energy, Comtrade strategic goods, CCFI). The output is an availability composition, not a risk score. National inputs are labelled national rather than shown as node values.
- **Stock Connect:** turnover is gross two-way activity and must not be read as net flow. The exchanges stopped publishing the buy/sell split on 2024-08-16.
